# Visitor's Guide to Port-Ysel

Port-Ysel is the only large town of the fictional Archipelago of Vaelmor. This short guide was written for travellers arriving in the year 1412 of the Lantern Era.

## Arriving

Ships dock at the Old Mole, the first quay of the town, built in the year 1203. During the High Water season, the Western Pass is closed to ships of more than forty tons, so large vessels must go around the island of Keld. The harbour master, Oswin Dacrel, can be found in the Hall of Ropes every morning.

## What to see

The Lighthouse of Keld can be visited by boat on clear days. The keeper, Bram Oskelle, welcomes guests and offers tea. In town, do not miss the shipyard of Ormesse, where the coaster Misty Gold was launched this year for the Grey Tides Company.

## What to eat

Try the seaweed soup of Brumeval, served with black bread and smoked silver herring. Visitors should avoid the salt cakes of Sarnoux, which are very strong.

## Practical advice

The council meets on the first day of each month in the Hall of Ropes, and most of its sessions are open to the public. Money is counted in deniers. A night in an inn costs about six deniers. If a ship arrives from an island hit by the grey fever, passengers must wait eleven days at anchor before landing.
