# Notes on the Vaelmor Tide Almanac

Working notes by the cartographer Maëlis Torvane on how to read the tide almanac of the archipelago.

## The forty-two day cycle

The Pale Gorge current reverses every forty-two days. Pilots of the Grey Tides Company use the almanac to know in advance which direction the current will run. A reversal in the middle of a crossing can add a full day to the journey between Keld and Sarnoux.

## Reading the almanac

Each page covers one week. The left column gives the height of the high tide at the Old Mole in Port-Ysel, and the right column gives the direction of the current. A small lantern symbol marks the days when the lighthouse of Keld shows its flashing light.

## Known errors

The edition of the year 1409 contains a mistake: it places the great tide of the autumn three days too early. Harbour master Oswin Dacrel ordered the printing house to correct the later editions.
